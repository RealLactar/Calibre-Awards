Only factual title/credit/year data is bundled; no source descriptions or
book excerpts are copied. Results explicitly name and link the secondary
historical archive, rather than implying the full facts were retrieved from
the registration-gated organizer archive. No network requests occur.

1978, 1981 and 1982 are omitted because the winner list credits Various authors.
1987, 1991 and 2017 are no-award years. Shortlists and the 1993/2008 Diagram
of Diagrams anniversary awards are not annual winners and are excluded.
Award year is retained, not announcement year (e.g. Cooking with Poo: 2011,
announced March 2012). No 2026 winner was verified at review time.

Author/editor credits are not nominators or publishers. The published name
Pat Califia is retained for 1990. All credited coauthors are required; order
and the Calibre ampersand separator are normalized. No publisher/author
substitution, fuzzy title matching, arbitrary subtitle stripping or single
coauthor match is permitted. Institution of Mechanical Engineers is the
explicit corporate credit for Designing High Performance Stiffened Structures.

Four explicitly documented full-title aliases are accepted: American Bottom
Archaeology, Bombproof Your Horse, How to Poo on a Date, and the organizer's
2025 spelling documented below. Other titles
must match the listed winning title, including its subtitle where listed.
Refresh clears RAM and reloads the bundled archive. Updates require a plugin
update. Award category is unset because this is a single title prize.
