# Bad Sex in Fiction historical facts

Reviewed 2026-10-03. The source ships factual titles, authors and award years,
not copyrighted excerpts. 28 book winners cover each year 1993–2019.

Official source: https://literaryreview.co.uk/bad-sex-in-fiction-award
Historical supplement: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literary_Review
Corroboration: https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Sex_in_Fiction_Award

The official overview lists selected years only and labels Philip Hook’s
The Stonebreakers as 1995. The historical sources agree on 1994 for that work
and 1995 for Philip Kerr’s Gridiron. These two records and omitted early years
explicitly retain the historical secondary source in their result attribution.
Other listed winners retain Literary Review attribution. 2019 joint winners
are separate title/author records. No rank is inferred. The official 2020
cancellation produces no result. No claims are made about awards after 2020.
Shortlists and John Updike’s author lifetime honor are deliberately excluded.
